# Erdős #678 — Formalization Correction (2026-08-14)

## Critical finding

During preparation of the second Lean test, direct arithmetic evaluation showed that the proposed triple

`(n,m,k) = (495,504,8)`

does **not** satisfy

`M(n,k) > M(m,k+1)`.

In fact:

`M(495,8) = 80724645896624814000`

and

`M(504,9) = 3184763995486432719360`,

so the inequality is reversed by a large factor.

## Root cause

The attempted rescued proof compared

`Q(t-k-1,k)`

on the interval `[t-k,t-1]` with

`Q(t,k+1)`

but then incorrectly treated the second interval as `[t,t+k]`.

By definition,

`M(t,k+1)` and its product numerator use

`[t+1,t+k+1]`,

not `[t,t+k]`.

Therefore the asserted identity

`Q(t,k+1) / Q(t-k-1,k) = L_k`

was not established for the actual problem intervals.

This invalidates the salvaged general construction.

## Status change

The proposed `p=5` construction is **REJECTED** as a proof of #678.

The Lean formalization must not encode it as a positive witness. Instead, it is retained as a regression test demonstrating that the proposed instance is false.

## Valid concrete witness retained

A separately verified numerical witness is:

`M(36,8) > M(47,9)`

with `47 >= 36 + 8`.

This is suitable for the next Lean test, but it proves only existence of one instance, not infinitude.

## Protocol lesson

Before formalizing a proposed witness generated by a symbolic construction, evaluate the exact defining expression numerically on the proposed parameters. In particular, audit interval endpoints after every substitution into `M(n,k)`, `P(n,k)`, and `Q(n,k)`.
