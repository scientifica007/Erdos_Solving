## Derived documentation lesson

Operational state synchronization and publication-narrative synchronization are distinct controls.

A project can have a correct restart checkpoint while a manuscript-oriented analytical document remains frozen at an earlier evidence cutoff. Publication-facing case studies should therefore declare an explicit evidence cutoff and be re-audited after later experiments or evaluation stages close.
